# Shooting the test clips

Two clips today for spikes 1–3, later 5–8 for evals. Only film stock you own or have written permission to
film. Keep customers and staff out of frame and out of earshot.

## Clip A: the normal case (45–60 s)
- 6–10 different products on a shelf or table (sarees, footwear, or kirana packs).
- Phone upright or sideways, either is fine. Walk slowly, about one product every 4–6 seconds.
- Say each price while the product is on screen, the way you would to a customer:
  "ye neela wala 1200", "cotton wala saadhe aath sau", "ye chappal 499".
- Shoot in the light you normally have, including bright window light.

## Clip B: the hard cases (30–60 s)
Put in on purpose, and write down what you did:
- Two items of the same colour next to each other, with different prices.
- One price said **before** the item appears, one said **after** you have moved past it.
- One price in Hindi words ("ek hazaar do sau", "dedh hazaar").
- One item with a visible price tag that **disagrees** with the price you say.
- One item with **no** price said.
- One folded or stacked textile pile.

## After shooting
1. Copy the files to `evals/videos/` (gitignored; they never go into git). Name them `clip_a.mp4` and
   `clip_b.mp4` (or `.webm`/`.mov`, whatever the phone gives).
2. Copy `evals/labels/_template.json` to `evals/labels/clip_a.json` (and `clip_b.json`) and fill in each
   product in the order it appears: what it is, the price you said, roughly when (seconds), and any tag
   price you could see. Approximate times are fine (±1 s).
