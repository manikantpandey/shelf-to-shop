# Privacy

What personal data Shelf-to-Shop handles, why, for how long, who else sees it, and how sellers control
it. Written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025: the seller
is the data principal for their own data; for their customers' order details the seller decides the
purpose and Shelf-to-Shop processes it for them.

## What we keep and why

| Data | Whose | Why | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shop name, WhatsApp number, language, profile (hours, address, delivery, UPI ID, photo, about, offer) | Seller | To run the seller's shop page and send orders to them | Until the seller deletes the shop |
| Shelf videos and their audio | Seller (may capture other people) | Only to find items and prices | Deleted after the retention period (Settings, default 7 days, 1 to 30) |
| Frames cut from videos (sampled frames, the frame behind each cut-out, moments the seller browsed) | Seller (may show people) | To make photos and show where a price came from | Deleted with the video |
| Item photos, names, prices, transcript text, price evidence | Seller | The catalog and its run details | Until the seller removes the item or deletes the shop |
| Customer name and note on an order (optional) | Seller's customer | So the seller knows who ordered and where to deliver | Cleared after 90 days; the items and total stay for the seller's records |
| Page views and order taps | Nobody (totals per day) | Shop statistics | Totals only, no visitor data at all |
| Session cookie | Seller | Keeping the seller signed in | 14 days, removed 30 days after it ends |
| Audit log | Seller (no personal data in entries) | Security and accountability | Kept for investigations |

The shop page never shows the seller's email, internal ids, videos, costs or price evidence.

## Consent and notice

- At setup: a plain notice of what is kept and why, before the seller agrees.
- Before the first recording: a separate notice that videos are only for making the catalog, are deleted
  after the retention period, and should not show customers or other people.
- The customer's basket says their name and note go to that shop only, with the order.
- Recording consent can be withdrawn (the API supports it today; a switch in Settings is planned);
  recording then stops until the seller agrees again. Deleting the shop removes everything.

## Who else processes data

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Meta Model API (Muse Spark, SAM 3.1, Muse Voice Transcribe, Muse Image) | The shelf video, its audio and frames, while a video is processed | Finding items, prices and photos. Contributor models that may train on content are refused; requests use `store: false`; uploaded files expire after one hour and are deleted after use |
| Open Food Facts | Product names or barcodes the seller searches | Finding pack photos (no personal data is sent) |
| WhatsApp (Meta) | The order message, sent by the customer from their own phone | The customer's choice to order; we never send messages ourselves |
| AWS (Mumbai region) | Everything above, on the server | Hosting |

## Seller rights

- **Download everything**: Settings, "Download all my data" gives a zip with the shop, profile,
  sections, items (with price evidence), orders and videos list as JSON, plus the item photos.
- **Correct**: every item, price, name and profile field can be edited at any time.
- **Delete everything**: Settings, "Delete my shop", typing the shop name to confirm. All rows and files
  are removed at once, including the published shop page; only an audit line without personal data stays.
- **Retention**: the seller chooses how many days videos are kept.

## AI-made content

Photos whose background was generated by Muse Image are marked AI-edited in the image metadata (IPTC
`DigitalSourceType` = `compositeSynthetic`) and labelled "AI background" on the shop page and in the
app (India IT Amendment Rules, 2026). The item pixels themselves are never changed. Pack photos from
Open Food Facts are credited with their licence (CC BY-SA 3.0).
