# Shoot plan: the day at the shop

Everything to film at the shop for the documentary in `docs/documentary-script.md`: who comes, what to
carry, the scenes in the order you shoot them (not the order of the film), every line people say or type,
the interview questions, and where the camera goes for each shot.

Laptop work (video details, Commerce Manager, SocialPulse, the narration) is done later at a desk and is
listed at the end.

---

## Who is needed

| Person | Job on the day |
|---|---|
| **Director** (you) | Runs the plan, asks the interview questions, checks every shot on the list |
| **Camera** | Films with phone A; a friend who can hold a phone steady is enough |
| **Shopkeeper** | Themselves: opens the shop, records the shelf, answers, packs the order |
| **Customer** | A friend with phone C, at home or round the corner; sends the WhatsApp messages and the order |
| Helper (optional) | Holds the light or reflector, keeps passers-by out of shot, logs shots |

## What to carry

- **Phone A (camera):** most storage free, 4K or 1080p at 30 fps, lens cleaned, fully charged.
- **Phone B (the shopkeeper's phone):** has the app open at https://shop.shankhyaai.com, screen recording
  switched on in quick settings, WhatsApp on it, do-not-disturb on (except WhatsApp).
- **Phone C (customer):** WhatsApp, the shopkeeper's number saved as a contact, screen recording on.
- A small tripod or phone clamp, a power bank and charging cables.
- Sound: a clip-on (lavalier) mic for phone A if you have one; otherwise record the interview with phone A
  close to the shopkeeper and a second phone running a voice recorder in their shirt pocket.
- Light: a white sheet or white foam board as a reflector; a small LED light if the shop is dim.
- **Two printed consent forms** (text at the end) and a pen.
- Water and chai money. The shopkeeper is giving you their morning.

## Before the day

1. **Ask the shopkeeper's permission** and agree a time: early morning, before the rush (about 6:30 to
   10:30 am).
2. **Set up their own shop in the app** with them, on phone B: open the site, **Start your own shop**,
   the shop's real name, agree to the notices, **their own WhatsApp number** (customers' orders will go
   there). Do not record anything yet.
3. **Pick 6 to 10 items** to film on one shelf: packets with clear labels work best. Note each item's
   real selling price with the shopkeeper. Never use a price above the printed MRP: it is not allowed.
4. **Pick one item whose price they will not say** in the video. The app will ask for it in Review, which
   gives the film its "it asks instead of guessing" moment honestly.
5. Check the customer's phone C can message phone B on WhatsApp.

## Scenes, in shooting order

Times are a guide. Hold every shot for at least 5 seconds, and shoot each important shot twice.

### Scene 1: The shutter opens (6:30, 15 min) — Cold open

**Action:** The shop is closed. The shopkeeper arrives, unlocks, rolls the shutter up, switches on the light.

| Shot | Angle | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | **Wide, from across the street**, camera on tripod at chest height | The whole shop front; the shopkeeper walks in from the side. Let the shutter go all the way up |
| 1b | **Low angle from inside the shop**, camera on the floor or a low shelf, pointing at the shutter | The shutter rising and morning light coming in. Best shot of the day; do two takes |
| 1c | **Close on hands**: the lock, the key, the light switch | 3–4 seconds each |

**Sound:** Record the shutter clearly; keep 10 seconds of street sound with no talking.

### Scene 2: The shop wakes up (6:45, 20 min) — Cold open and Act 1 b-roll

**Action:** The shopkeeper does their real morning routine: straightening packets, filling the counter jar,
wiping the counter.

| Shot | Angle | How |
|---|---|---|
| 2a | **Close, side-on** | Hands straightening a strip of snack packets |
| 2b | **Top-down** (phone held flat above the counter) | Coins, the notebook (bahi), the scale |
| 2c | **Macro on price stickers and printed MRPs** | Slow push-in, 5 seconds |
| 2d | **Slow pan along the shelves**, phone at shelf height | Left to right, very slow; two takes |

### Scene 3: The price questions arrive (7:05, 15 min) — Cold open and Act 1

**Action:** Phone B lies on the counter. The customer, from phone C, sends these messages one by one,
about 10 seconds apart, the first one with a blurry photo of a shelf:

> 1. "Bhaiya, ye wala kitne ka hai?" *(with the blurry photo)*
> 2. "Aur Punjabi tadka hai kya? Kitne ka?"
> 3. "Bhaiya reply karo please"

**Shopkeeper:** sees the phone light up, sighs, picks it up, starts typing a price with one thumb while
holding a packet in the other hand. No line to say.

| Shot | Angle | How |
|---|---|---|
| 3a | **Close, phone on the counter, slightly from above** | The screen lighting up with each message; focus on the phone |
| 3b | **Over the shoulder** of the shopkeeper | The phone in their hand, the shelf behind |
| 3c | **Screen recording on phone B** | The three messages arriving (crisp insert for the edit) |

### Scene 4: The interview (7:20, 25 min) — Act 1

**Setup:** The shopkeeper behind the counter, shelves behind them. Camera at their eye level, on a tripod,
one step to the side of the director. The shopkeeper looks at the **director**, not at the lens. Light
from the shop front falls on their face; hold the white sheet on the darker side. Clip the mic on. Phone A
on **medium shot** (waist up); shoot one take wider and one closer if you can.

**Questions** (ask in Hindi, one at a time; let them finish; leave a second of silence after each answer):

1. "Aapki dukaan kab se hai? Kya kya rakhte hain?"
   *(How long has the shop been here, what do you stock?)* Warm-up; probably not used.
2. "WhatsApp pe customer aapse kya poochhte hain?"
   *(What do customers ask you on WhatsApp?)*
3. "Ek din mein kitne aise message aate hain? Sabka jawaab kaise dete ho?"
   *(How many such messages a day? How do you answer them all?)*
4. "Kabhi catalog ya list banane ki koshish ki? Kya hua?"
   *(Did you ever try to make a catalog or list? What happened?)*
5. "Agar sab cheezon ki photo aur daam phone pe daalne ho, kitna time lagega?"
   *(If you had to put every item's photo and price on the phone, how long would it take?)*

**Follow-ups when an answer is good:** "Thoda aur batao" (tell me more), "Ek example do" (give an example).

**Rules:** do not hand them lines or ask them to repeat your words. If an answer is short, ask the same
question a different way later. Ask question 6 only after Scene 8:

6. "Pehli baar video se apni dukaan online dekhi, kaisa laga?"
   *(You saw your shop online from a video for the first time: how did it feel?)*

### Scene 5: Recording the shelf (7:50, 20 min) — Act 2, the hero scene

**Action:** On phone B, the shopkeeper opens the app, taps **Record**, agrees to the recording notice, and
walks slowly past the shelf for 25–40 seconds, pointing the phone at each item and saying its price the way
they would to a customer. **Make sure no customer is in frame.**

**What they say** (their own words; this is only the pattern, with the real prices filled in):

> "Ye Haldiram ka Nut Cracker, chaar rupaye.
> Ye salted peanut, ye bhi chaar ka.
> Punjabi tadka, same price.
> Ye Good Day biscuit, das rupaye.
> *(one item shown clearly but its price not said)*
> Ye Pepsi wali bottle, pandrah ki."

Keep it natural: Hindi, English or mixed is all fine. Slow walking, about two seconds per item, each item
held in frame while its price is said.

| Shot | Angle | How |
|---|---|---|
| 5a | **Over the shoulder**, a little above | The phone screen with the red record ring **and** the shelf behind it. The key shot; two takes (only one goes to the app) |
| 5b | **Side profile, medium**, from the end of the aisle | The shopkeeper walking slowly, speaking, phone up |
| 5c | **Screen recording on phone B** | The app's recorder: the ring, "Move slowly", "Say each price", the mic level |
| 5d | **Close on their mouth and hand** | Saying one price; lip and hand only |

**Then:** tap stop, **Use this video**, and let it upload. Processing takes 2–3 minutes.

### Scene 6: The wait (8:10, 5 min) — b-roll

While the app reads the video: the shopkeeper serves the first customer of the day (only with that
customer's consent; otherwise film only hands and the counter), sips chai, glances at the phone.

| Shot | Angle | How |
|---|---|---|
| 6a | **Close on phone B** | The step list filling in ("Finding items", "Hearing prices"...) |
| 6b | **Medium, from outside looking in** | The shopkeeper at the counter, phone in hand |

### Scene 7: Checking and publishing (8:15, 15 min) — Act 3 and Act 4

**Action:** Review opens. The app asks for the price it did not hear. The shopkeeper types it, checks the
items and photos, and taps **Publish**.

**Shopkeeper's reaction:** whatever they really say. If they read the screen aloud, keep it.

| Shot | Angle | How |
|---|---|---|
| 7a | **Over the shoulder, close** | The question card, the thumb typing the price |
| 7b | **Screen recording on phone B** | Review, the question answered, the price tag turning yellow, Publish |
| 7c | **Close on the face** | Their reaction when the items appear |

### Scene 8: The customer orders (8:30, 20 min) — Act 4

**Action, in the shop:** The shopkeeper taps **Share on WhatsApp** and sends the shop link to the customer.

**Action, at the customer's place:** The customer opens the link on phone C, scrolls the shop, taps **Add**
on two items (one tapped twice), opens the basket, fills in:

> Your name: **Asha** *(or the customer's own first name)*
> Note for the shop: **"Shaam 6 baje le jaungi"** *(I'll pick it up at 6 this evening)*

taps **Send order on WhatsApp**, and in WhatsApp taps **Send**.

| Shot | Angle | How |
|---|---|---|
| 8a | **Close on phone B** in the shop | The shopkeeper sending the link |
| 8b | **Over the customer's shoulder**, on a sofa or at a doorway | Phone C with the shop open; tapping Add |
| 8c | **Screen recording on phone C** | The whole order: shop, basket, form, WhatsApp opening with the order written out and the total |
| 8d | **Close on phone B in the shop** | The order arriving with the ping |

Film the customer from behind or hands only, unless they have signed a consent form.

### Scene 9: Packing the order (8:50, 15 min) — Act 4

**Action:** The shopkeeper reads the order aloud to themselves (their words), takes the items off the shelf,
puts them in a bag, writes the order number on it, and taps **Mark packed** in Orders.

| Shot | Angle | How |
|---|---|---|
| 9a | **Medium**, from across the counter | Reading the order, reaching for the items |
| 9b | **Close on hands** | Items going into the bag, the number written on it |
| 9c | **Screen recording on phone B** | Orders: the order card, Mark packed |

**Now ask interview question 6** (Scene 4 setup).

### Scene 10: The Diwali sale (9:15, 15 min) — Act 4

**Action:** In **Offers**, the shopkeeper picks **Diwali**, 10% or the discount they really want to give,
**All items** or one section, the last day, and taps **Start offer**. Then **Tell customers on WhatsApp**
and sends it to their Status or a broadcast list. If the shop has real Diwali decorations up, include them;
do not add props.

| Shot | Angle | How |
|---|---|---|
| 10a | **Screen recording on phone B** | Offers form, Start offer, the running offer, the WhatsApp message |
| 10b | **Over the shoulder** | Tapping Tell customers on WhatsApp |
| 10c | **Screen recording on phone C** | The shop page with the sale banner and the new prices |

### Scene 11: The closing shot (9:35, 15 min) — closing shot

**Action:** The shopkeeper serving a customer at the counter (consent signed), smiling, ordinary.

| Shot | Angle | How |
|---|---|---|
| 11a | **Medium, from inside behind the shelf**, between packets | The counter through a gap in the stock |
| 11b | **Slow pull-back** from the counter to the street | Walk backwards slowly, phone at chest height, ending on the shop front. Two or three takes |

### Scene 12: Sound only (9:50, 5 min)

Phone A on the counter, everyone quiet: **30 seconds of shop sound**, **the shutter** once more, and the
**WhatsApp ping** on phone B five times. The editor will thank you.

**Wrap by about 10:00.** Thank the shopkeeper; send them the shop link.

## Camera angles at a glance

| Name | Where the camera is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Wide | Far, the whole place in frame | Opening, the shop front, the ending |
| Medium | Waist up | Interview, packing, serving |
| Close | Hands, face or one object fills the frame | Emotion and detail |
| Insert | Very close on one thing (a sticker, a coin, a screen) | Short cutaways |
| Over the shoulder | Behind the person, their phone and what they look at both visible | Recording, reviewing, ordering |
| Top-down | Phone flat above the counter | Coins, notebook, packing |
| Low angle | Near the floor, looking up | The shutter rising |
| Side profile | From the side, level | Walking along the shelf |

**Rules:** hold every shot 5 seconds; move slowly or not at all; keep the light behind the camera, not
behind the person; lock focus and exposure by pressing on the subject; shoot horizontally (16:9), except a
few extra vertical shots of Scenes 5, 8 and 10 for the Reels cut.
