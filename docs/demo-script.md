# Demo video script (3 minutes)

For recording the hackathon demo. Everything shown is the live site, https://shop.shankhyaai.com, and real
model output; nothing is mocked.

## Before you record

- **Laptop** (1440 px or wider browser): open https://shop.shankhyaai.com in a fresh private window.
- **Phone**: Android with Chrome, screen recording on. For the Record scene, film your own shelf (no
  customers in frame) and say each price out loud in Hinglish.
- **Second phone or WhatsApp Web as the customer**, so the order message is sent to the shop's number from
  someone else. Sending to your own number also works ("Message yourself"), but looks less real.
- Optional: the Commerce Manager tab with the "Shelf-to-Shop test" catalogue open on Catalogue → Products.
- Close other tabs and notifications. Zoom the browser to 110% so text reads on a small video.

## Tips

- Say less than you think: the screens explain themselves. Keep each sentence to one idea.
- If a model takes a while on the live recording, cut from "upload" to the finished review; say "about two
  minutes later". Do not speed up or fake output.
- If the newest sample video has a price question instead of a photo question, use it: tap the right price.
  That is the strongest moment.
- Leave the demo order unsent if you only have one phone, and show the WhatsApp screen with the message
  ready: it proves the flow without messaging yourself.
